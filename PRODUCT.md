# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Engineers, researchers, philosophical debaters, and curious thinkers seeking truth, dialectical rigor, and privacy. Users want real-time, offline, local-first argument critique and fallacy identification without streaming sensitive audio to cloud providers.

## Product Purpose
Elenchus (ἔλεγχος) is an offline debate analysis and logical fallacy detection engine. It transcribes spoken debate locally on CPU via faster-whisper, evaluates rhetorical structures and informal fallacies via local LLMs (Ollama), and delivers immediate Socratic counter-arguments through a frameless desktop HUD overlay.

## Brand Personality
- **Editorial-tech**: Restrained, deliberate, precise; a wireframe rendered in light on obsidian.
- **Quiet authority**: 400-weight typography communicating hierarchy through scale and tracking alone, never shouting.
- **Socratic dialectic**: Relentless inquiry, intellectual discipline, calm and unshakeable rigor.

## Anti-references
- Neon purple/cyan SaaS gradients and decorative gradient text (`background-clip: text`).
- Generic glassmorphism and decorative blur soup.
- Cluttered AI chatbot interfaces and cartoonish icons.
- Noisy, unmotivated bounce or elastic animations.
- Eyebrow tropes and decorative numbered headers on every section.

## Design Principles
1. **Blueprint in Light**: Carve negative space out of obsidian with 1px hairline borders (`#212121`) and crisp typography (`#f3f3f3`), accented only by Compass Gold (`#6f6759`) and Pulse Green (`#98ff38`).
2. **Authority Through Scale, Not Shouting**: Rely on oversized 400-weight headings with negative tracking rather than bold screaming.
3. **Show the Mechanism**: Expose the live HUD simulator, audio energy monochart, latency budget, and cryptographic release hashes directly.
4. **Accessible Rigor**: Maintain strict WCAG AA contrast (≥4.5:1), complete keyboard accessibility, and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Target: WCAG 2.1 AA compliance across all surfaces.
- Text contrast: Body text and metadata ≥4.5:1 against `#101010` and `#080808` backgrounds.
- Motion accommodations: Full `prefers-reduced-motion` fallbacks across canvas particles and pulse animations.
- Keyboard navigation: Clear, high-contrast focus rings on all interactive elements.
