Final design

The Faceted Caliper

A mirrored-3 lowercase Greek polytonic epsilon (ἔ) formed from 45-degree chamfered precision caliper facets.

  • Typographic authenticity: Direct representation of the Greek root ἔλεγχος (elenchus) crowned with smooth breathing (psili) and acute spark (oxia)
  • Instrumental aesthetic: 45-degree architectural chamfers evoke high-end CNC-milled obsidian hardware, perfectly harmonizing with the dark desktop HUD
  • Scale resilience: 100/100 audited vector geometry that stays razor-sharp from 16px favicons to high-res display billboards
